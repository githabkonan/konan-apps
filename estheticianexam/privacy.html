Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how "Esthetician Exam: Board Prep" (the "App") handles information. The developer is an independent individual publishing under the alias "Konan".

1. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. The App does not require sign-in, an account, an email address, or any other identifying information. It does not use any analytics SDK, ad SDK, or third-party tracker.

All learning progress (session history, missed questions, streak counter) is stored only on your device via local storage. Nothing is transmitted to us or to any server we operate.

2. In-App Purchase

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive or store any payment information. Purchase state is preserved locally on your device and can be restored on the same Apple ID via "Restore Purchase" inside the App.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Because no personal data is collected, none is shared with any third party. There is no advertising and no third-party analytics.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Deleting the App from your device removes all local data. To request additional information, contact us at the email below.

5. Children

The App is intended for adult learners preparing for a professional licensing exam. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Security

Local data is protected by iOS standard on-device encryption. Communications with the App Store are secured by Apple over HTTPS/TLS.
