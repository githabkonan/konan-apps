Frequently Asked Questions

What does the purchase include?
A single one-time payment unlocks all practice questions across the six SCFHS Radiographer domains (Radiation Physics & Protection, Image Production, Radiographic Procedures, Patient Care & Safety, Equipment Operation & QA, Professional Ethics & KSA Healthcare Regulations), the three timed 100-question mock exams, per-domain drills, and one-line rationales. No subscription, no ads.
I changed iPhones. Do I need to buy again?
No. Sign in with the same Apple ID and reinstall — the App Store treats the purchase as already owned. Progress (per-device local) does not carry over between devices; the purchase itself does.
Is my progress synced across devices?
Progress (seen questions, per-domain accuracy, mock scores) is stored locally on each device. Deleting the app clears that device's progress. Your purchase can always be restored from the App Store.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. The app works 100% offline. You can practice on the metro, at a clinic between shifts, or anywhere without signal.
Is this affiliated with SCFHS or Prometric?
No. This app is an independent, unofficial study aid. Questions are original items authored from the publicly available SCFHS Radiographer blueprint (domain-level structure only, no literal reproductions of Prometric items). Not affiliated with the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties or with Prometric.
Does passing the mock exams guarantee I pass the real Prometric exam?
No study aid can guarantee a passing score. Mock A/B/C are designed to reflect the six SCFHS domains and the real 120-minute Prometric window. Consistent 70%+ mock scores generally indicate solid readiness, but individual results vary.
Is this medical advice?
No. Every question and rationale is a study aid. Nothing in the app is a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or patient-facing medical advice.
How do I request a refund?
App Store refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
