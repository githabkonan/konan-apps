Privacy Policy — SCFHS Radiographer Prep

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application "SCFHS Radiographer Prep" (the "App") handles your information. The developer is an independent individual publishing under the alias "Konan".

1. Data we collect

We collect no personal data. The App does not require sign-in, an account, an email address, or any other identifying information. It does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.

All study progress (seen question ids, per-domain accuracy, mock scores, streak counter, chosen language) is stored only on your device via local storage. Nothing is transmitted to us or to any server we operate.

2. In-app purchase

The App is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple processes the payment via StoreKit. We do not receive or store payment information. Your purchase can be restored on the same Apple ID by reinstalling the App.

  • Apple Inc. — App Store payment processing. Apple Privacy Policy

3. Third parties

Because we collect no personal data, no information is shared with any third party. There are no third-party ads or analytics.

4. Retention and deletion

Removing the App from your device deletes all local study data. For any additional request, email us at the address below.

5. Minors

The App is aimed at adult radiographers and X-Ray technicians preparing for the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS) Prometric licensing exam. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Security

Local data is protected by iOS standard on-device encryption. Communications with the App Store are protected by Apple via HTTPS/TLS.

7. Changes to this Policy

If we modify this Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the bottom of this page.
