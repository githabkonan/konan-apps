Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the app "KEIEI The beginning" (the "App") handles information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information. No account or sign-in is required. It does not obtain your name, address, email address, phone number, location, or advertising identifier (IDFA). It uses no analytics tools and no advertising SDKs.

2. Network

All game data is bundled with the App. It does not communicate with external servers while you play, and it works in airplane mode.

3. Device features

The App does not use the microphone, camera, location, photos, contacts, or notifications.

4. Purchases

The App is a one-time purchase on the App Store. There are no in-app purchases and no ads. Payment is handled by Apple; the developer never receives your payment details.

5. Sharing with third parties

Because no personal information is collected, none is shared with third parties.

6. External links

Tapping "Privacy Policy" or "Support" in the App opens the corresponding page of this site in Safari.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date on this page will be revised.
