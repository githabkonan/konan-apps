KEIEI The beginning — Support

A first-person 3D horror game set in the guardhouse of an army base late at night. This game is entirely fictional and has no connection to any real person, organization, unit, or base.

How to play

  • Drag on the left side of the screen to move, and on the right side to look around.
  • Walk up to doors, the gate, the security monitor, and the chair, then tap them.
  • When someone comes to the gate, walk up to them. Once they hold up their ID, tap them to check it.
  • We recommend playing in landscape with earphones.

FAQ

Q. Is this a subscription?
No. It is a one-time purchase on the App Store. There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

Q. Do I need an internet connection?
No. All data is included in the app, so you can play offline.

Q. If I die, do I start over?
Once you pass a checkpoint in the story, you restart from that checkpoint. If you quit the app, you start from the beginning.

Q. Is there frightening content?
Yes. The game contains blood and sudden scares. Please be careful if you are sensitive to these.

Q. There is no sound.
Turn up the volume on your device. Sound plays even when the silent switch is on.

Q. Which languages are supported?
Japanese and English. The game follows your device language.
