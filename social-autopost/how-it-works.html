How it works

A single-operator pipeline that publishes my own promo videos for my own published apps, through official platform APIs.

What the tool does

I publish several exam-preparation and utility apps on the Apple App Store (see Apps). To market them, I record short promotional videos. Instead of uploading the same clips by hand every day, I built social-autopost: a small program that keeps a queue of my own promo videos and posts the next one to my own connected social channels on a fixed daily schedule.

It is used by exactly one person — me, the developer and owner of the connected accounts. It does not sign up other users, does not accept third-party content, and does not post to anyone else's account.

The publishing flow

  • Authorize once. I connect my own account through the platform's official OAuth flow and grant only the scopes needed to upload a video.
  • Queue. A small local queue holds my own short promo clips, each with its caption and the target app it advertises.
  • Scheduled post. On a fixed daily schedule, the tool takes the next clip and sends it to the platform through its official upload/publish endpoint.
  • Nothing else. It does not read feeds, follow/unfollow, comment, message, scrape, or touch any other user's data.

TikTok integration

For TikTok, the tool uses the Content Posting API with the Direct Post capability to publish a video to my own authorized TikTok profile, and Login Kit to authenticate my own account. The requested scopes are:

scopeWhat it is used for
user.info.basicRead my own basic profile (open id, display name) to confirm which authorized account will receive the post.
video.uploadUpload my own promo clip to my own account as a draft.
video.publishDirectly post my own promo clip to my own authorized TikTok profile.

Every request is made for my own account only, using content I created to promote my own published apps. Authorization can be revoked at any time from my TikTok account settings.

Other connected platforms

The same pattern applies to my other own channels: authenticate my own account via the official API, then publish my own promo clips through the official upload endpoint on a schedule. No browser automation and no unofficial endpoints are used anywhere.

Summary for reviewers: single operator, own accounts only, own promotional content only, official APIs only, minimal scopes, revocable at any time. Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy; acceptable use in the Terms of Service.
