Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how social-autopost ("the application", "we", "I") handles information. social-autopost is a personal automation tool operated solely by its developer to publish the developer's own promotional videos to the developer's own connected social media channels. It is not offered to other users.

1. Information the application accesses

  • Google / YouTube account authorization. With your explicit consent through Google's OAuth 2.0 flow, the application is granted the YouTube Data API v3 youtube.upload permission. This permission is used only to upload videos to your own YouTube channel.
  • The application does not request or access your viewing history, subscriptions, comments, other users' data, or any personal profile information beyond what is strictly required to upload to your own channel.

2. How information is used

  • To upload the operator's own short promotional video files to the operator's own YouTube channel on a fixed schedule.
  • The application performs no other processing of Google user data.

3. Storage and security

  • The OAuth refresh token issued by Google is stored only on the operator's own local machine, in a restricted-access configuration file. It is never transmitted to any third party or external server.
  • No Google user data is collected into any database, analytics system, or shared service.

4. Sharing of information

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. The application has no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no external recipients.

5. Limited Use disclosure

social-autopost's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The application's access to YouTube APIs is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access

You may revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time through the Google Account permissions page (Security → Third-party apps with account access). Upon revocation, the stored token becomes invalid and the application can no longer upload to your channel.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the application stores only a single OAuth token locally and no other user data, deletion is accomplished by revoking access (see above) and removing the local configuration file. No retained copies exist elsewhere.

8. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
