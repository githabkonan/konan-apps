How it works

A small, unattended pipeline so I don't hand-upload the same promo clips every day.

1

Queue my clips

I keep a small queue of short videos that promote my own published apps, each with its caption.

2

Scheduled upload

On a fixed daily schedule the tool takes the next clip and uploads it to my own connected channels via each platform's official API.

3

Official APIs only

Publishing goes through official, authorized endpoints — no browser automation, no scraping, no third-party accounts.

Scope & compliance

Single operator. This is not a public product and does not onboard other users. It is used solely by me, the developer, to manage my own marketing content for my own apps.

Own account, own data. With my explicit authorization, the tool posts only to my own connected channels. It does not read, modify, or access any other user's data.

Official APIs. Each platform is accessed through its official API and documented scopes. Access can be revoked at any time from the connected account's settings. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
