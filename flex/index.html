10億円を使い切る

投資・FX・事業

THE FLEX (English)

You wake up one morning to find ¥1 billion in your bank account. THE FLEX is a spending simulation game where you burn through a fictional fortune by shopping, investing, trading FX, and running a business. Run low and you can earn it back by working one of 12 jobs. Play in Normal mode (start with ¥1 billion) or Hard mode (start with ¥0), in English or Japanese.

  • All in-game money is fictional and cannot be cashed out.
  • No ads. Your game data is stored only on your device.
  • THE FLEX is a paid app with optional in-app purchases (PREMIUM / LEGENDARY, FlexCoin packs, developer tips). All purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. See the App Store product page for the price.
