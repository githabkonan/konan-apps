Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04 · Polski

1. Who is the data controller

The data controller is Konan, an independent developer who makes the app Testy PSP (English App Store name: PSP Exam Prep: Fire Service; bundle ID com.konan.psptesty). Contact address: konakonan822@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

No personal data is collected, transmitted or processed by our developers or by any third party.

  • The app does not require registration or login.
  • The app contains no ads and no analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no trackers).
  • The app makes no network requests — all content is built into the app package and works offline.
  • Your study progress and the history of your wrong answers are saved only locally on your device (localStorage). We have no access to this data.

3. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

The app does not track your activity and does not connect to advertising networks, so it does not show the ATT prompt required by iOS 14.5+.

4. Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the State Fire Service (Państwowa Straż Pożarna) exam. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

5. Payments

The app is a one-time purchase, at the price set on the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we have no access to your financial information or payment method.

6. Changes to this policy

Changes will be published at this URL. If a change affects how data is handled, we will announce it in the update notes on the App Store.
