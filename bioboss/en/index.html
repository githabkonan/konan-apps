Life Control

Life Control turns healthy habits into a game. Your daily choices shape the person you become years from now.

What it does

  • Health Age simulator — answer 23 quick lifestyle questions and see your estimated health age
  • Daily AI coaching on the one habit worth changing first, and why
  • Daily missions built around sleep, movement, hydration and posture
  • Add your own missions and rules you want to break
  • Levels, HP and streaks to keep the habit going

All health data stays on your device and is never sent to a server. No ads. One-time purchase, no subscription.
