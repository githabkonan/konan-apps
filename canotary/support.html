Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What can I try for free?
A. You can preview the first 30 questions per category with full explanations before deciding to unlock.
Q. What does the unlock include?
A. A one-time $6.99 purchase unlocks all 200 practice questions across every content area of the California Notary Public Handbook, three timed 60-question mock exams, category-by-category drills, and full Handbook-cited explanations. No subscription. No auto-renewal.
Q. I changed phones. Do I need to buy again?
A. No. Sign into the same Apple ID and tap "Restore Purchase" in the paywall screen to restore your unlock at no cost.
Q. Is my learning progress synced across devices?
A. Progress is stored locally on each device. Deleting the App resets progress on that device. Your purchase can always be restored.
Q. Does the App cover the most recent Handbook edition?
A. The question bank is written against the current California Secretary of State Notary Public Handbook. Every explanation cites the Handbook page or California Government / Civil Code section it comes from. If the Handbook is revised, the App will be updated in a subsequent release.
Q. Is this an official California Secretary of State product?
A. No. The App is an independent study aid. Questions are original items authored from the publicly published California Notary Public Handbook and California statutes. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the California Secretary of State.
Q. Does passing the mock exams guarantee I pass the real test?
A. No study aid can guarantee a passing score. The mock exams are timed to match the state exam format and drawn from the Handbook content areas. Consistent 80%+ scores across all three mock exams typically indicate exam readiness, but personal results will vary.
