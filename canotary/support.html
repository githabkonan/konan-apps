Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How much does the App cost? Are there in-app purchases?
A. The App is a one-time paid download. The current price is shown on the App Store product page. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no ads.
Q. What is included?
A. Everything is included from the start: 200 practice questions across 8 subject areas of the California Notary Public Handbook, 10-question practice sets, category-by-category drills, three 60-question mock exams, a Journal Entry Trainer, and a Maximum Fee Calculator. Each question has an explanation with a reference to the Handbook page it is based on.
Q. I changed phones. Do I need to buy again?
A. No. Sign in with the same Apple ID and download the App again from the App Store at no extra cost.
Q. Is my learning progress synced across devices?
A. No. Progress is stored locally on each device. Deleting the App resets progress on that device.
Q. Does the App cover the most recent Handbook edition?
A. The question bank is based on the California Secretary of State Notary Public Handbook. Every explanation cites the Handbook page or California Government / Civil Code section it comes from. If the Handbook is revised, the App will be updated in a subsequent release.
Q. Is this an official California Secretary of State product?
A. No. The App is an independent study aid. Questions are original items authored from the publicly published California Notary Public Handbook and California statutes. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the California Secretary of State.
Q. Does passing the mock exams guarantee I pass the real test?
A. No study aid can guarantee a passing score. Each mock exam has 60 questions drawn at random from all subject areas. Use your scores to find weak areas, but personal results will vary.
